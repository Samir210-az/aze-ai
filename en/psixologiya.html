ABA Therapy

AI Test

12 clinical questionnaires in Azerbaijani and Russian. Results are stored only in the user's browser.

03

Course Portal

Three online courses for specialists on one portal: Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (20 modules), Child and Adolescent Psychology (15 topics) and Child and Adolescent Therapy Programs (13 programs). Each course is built session by session, sign-in is by PIN.

3 courses · 20 modules · 15 topics · 13 programs

04

Test and Course Catalog

A catalog of tests and courses. For each test it is written separately what it measures and what it consists of.

Catalog · Description

05

Assessment System

Observation items and a detailed report. The official test score is optional and does not affect the observation results.

ADOS-2 · ADI-R · WISC-V · Vineland-3 · Leiter-3 · Sensory Profile 2

06

GOBDÖ-2

A calculator for the Gilliam autism rating scale. Separate reports for the specialist and the parent, with comparison to the previous result.

Licensed

07

Career Choice Test

For teenagers aged 14–16: interests (RIASEC), abilities and values. Only the psychologist sees the result and prints it for the parent.

AZ · RU

08

Individual Rehabilitation Plan

The specialist builds a 30-day plan for the child: the number of sessions per week is chosen and each session lasts 45 minutes. It works by rules and does not use AI.

Rule-based

09

Therapy Journal

Session notes, weekly results and a certificate for the parent. The certificate is prepared by an AI assistant.

Sessions · Weekly result · Certificate

10

TTAP Professional Plus

A multi-domain assessment for typically developing children and children with special needs. The result shows a suitable education area and vocational direction.

Domains · Vocational direction

11

IQ TEST

An original non-verbal reasoning test with three forms for ages 3–15.

Pilot stage

12

CBT Clinical Assessment

A clinical assessment tool based on the cognitive behavioural approach.

Licensed

13

Service Packages

A page presenting the centre's 4 service packages. The parent sees the packages side by side, reads what each one covers and applies.

4 packages · Application

14

AN Mərkəz saytı

The website of a centre providing community-based integration and remote support for children with special needs. Three simple steps are explained, from application to programme.

Remote support

15

M-CHAT-R

An autism screening test: the parent answers the questions, the result is shown as low, medium or high risk and the next step is written. In Azerbaijani, Russian and English, with administration guidance for the specialist.

AZ · RU · EN · Screening

16

Denver II

Developmental screening for children aged 0–6: more than 120 items, an age filter and a detailed report. The administration guide is written in plain language and past tests are kept in an archive.

120+ items · Archive

17

Developmental Route

A professional template for early development assessment: child code, age, history and a profile by area. Completed tests are saved, and each result opens and downloads as a separate HTML file. It is not a standardised test.

Profile · HTML result

18

Early Intervention

An early intervention and development platform. The parent signs in with email or an SMS code and adds children; a specialist's registration is approved by the admin. The Premium package opens a detailed analysis of screening results by area.

Parent · Specialist · Premium

19

Parent Rehabilitation Guide

An individual daily guide only for the parent's work with the child at home. The plan is split into morning, day and evening, and works as a single HTML file, even offline.

Daily plan · Offline

20

SCARED Screening

The 41-item SCARED questionnaire for checking anxiety in children and adolescents. The result is scored with the Birmaher key, and areas such as panic, separation anxiety and social anxiety are shown separately.

41 items · Birmaher

21

NeuroCBT Klinik

A clinical platform for the BAI (anxiety) and BDI (depression) tests. It is for licensed specialists, and the result shows symptom severity and does not give a diagnosis.

BAI · BDI · Licence

22

My Voice

A card-based communication app for children with limited speech. Tapping a card speaks the word, and sentences can be built. It has Azerbaijani, Russian and English, cards are added in parent mode, and ARASAAC pictograms are used.

AZ · RU · EN · Cards

23

Psychology Agent

An AI assistant for child psychology: you type a question or say it by voice and get an answer in a conversation.

AI chat · Voice

24

Data Base

A system for the centre's child registration, therapy sessions and statistical reports. Gemini AI looks at a child's results and suggests a weekly plan.

Registration · Session · AI plan

25

School Readiness

A portal for mental, emotional and social preparation of children aged 5–6. An 8-week programme, interactive lessons and daily home tasks; the teacher keeps daily notes and the parent follows progress.

8 weeks · Home tasks

26

Russian Language Course

A Russian-language learning site for children aged 7–12: alphabet, 24 lessons and a weekly study plan.

24 lessons · Alphabet

27

Parent Academy

An online training platform for parents of children with autism, ADHD, speech delay, sensory problems and behavioural difficulties. It teaches correct intervention at home with video, PDF and image materials and does not replace a therapist.

Video · PDF · Parent training

28

Clinic System

Management for centres and practices: patient registration, session scheduling, staff, payments, expenses and reports. Admin and staff roles are separate, and operations are kept in an audit log. A demo version for presentations is also available.

Patients · Sessions · Payments · Audit
